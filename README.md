## 👩‍💻 About Me
Hi! I'm Ximena, a developer-in-the-making from Madrid. I study Multiplatform Application Development, and what I enjoy most is that moment when something that made zero sense for days suddenly clicks. I'm not a "read the docs once and get it" person: I learn by building small things, breaking them, and slowly figuring out why they broke.

Outside of the screen-for-class side of life, I'm usually on a screen for fun instead. Video games are a big part of who I am, and music is always playing in the background, whether I'm coding, playing or just existing. I like things that are well made, from a clean function to a good track to a game with a ridiculous amount of detail.

I'm still figuring a lot of things out, and honestly I'm enjoying the process.
